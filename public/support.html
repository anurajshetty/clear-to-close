Support

Clear to Close keeps you and your realtor connected and up to date, from accepted offer to closing. If you need a hand, start with the answers below, or write to us.

Email us: support@miralabz.com
We reply to support requests by email, normally within one business day. If your message is about a specific escrow, please include the property address so we can find it quickly. Please don’t send passwords or one-time codes by email.

For realtors

  • Getting started. Sign up with your email, then confirm your account from the confirmation email (check spam if it doesn’t arrive). Complete your profile so your name and photo appear properly for your team and clients.
  • Inviting your team. Open an escrow and use the Team screen to invite one Associate and one Transaction Coordinator by email. They sign in with their own accounts and accept the invitation. Invitations only work while the escrow is open.
  • Sharing with clients. Use the Share screen to give each buyer or seller their one-time code. A code works on one device; if a client gets a new phone or the code stops working, generate a fresh code from the same screen — the checklist they see is always the escrow’s current state.
  • Notifications. Allow notifications when the app asks, so escrow updates reach your phone. You can change this anytime in your phone’s Settings.
  • Password help. Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen. For security, the reset message is the same whether or not the email has an account.

For buyers and sellers

  • Opening your escrow. Your realtor will send you a one-time code. Open the app link, enter the code, and you’ll land on your escrow, where you can follow every step as it’s checked off.
  • Code not working? Codes are single-use and tied to one device. If yours doesn’t work, ask your realtor to generate a new code for you from their Share screen.
  • Something looks wrong on your escrow? Your realtor manages the checklist and dates, so message them first — and you’re welcome to write to us as well.

Your account and privacy

  • Read how we handle information in our Privacy Policy.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your information, or to delete your account, email support@miralabz.com from the address on the account and we’ll take care of it and confirm by email.
