Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Clear to Close (“the app”) is an escrow tracking service for real estate professionals and their clients, operated by Anuraj Shetty (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Clear to Close, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When a real estate professional creates an account, we collect their email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Profile information. Professionals may add a profile: name, brokerage, phone number, real estate license (DRE) number, photo, banner image, biography, years of experience, areas served, and links to review pages.
  • Transaction information. Professionals enter escrow details to run their transactions: property addresses and property details, buyer and seller names, key dates, checklist progress, intake form information (which can include contact details for transaction parties and lenders, and property history provided for a sale), team invitations (colleague email addresses and roles), and activity history on each escrow.
  • Client access. Buyers, sellers, and coordinators who open an escrow through an invitation use a one-time code rather than an account. We record the invitation, the party name on it, and a device identifier for the device that redeemed it, so the link keeps working on that device and can be revoked by the professional.
  • Reviews. Clients may leave a star rating and a written review for their realtor, which is shown on the realtor’s profile.
  • Notifications. If a professional enables notifications, we store a push notification token for their device so we can deliver escrow updates. We also send transactional email (account confirmation, invitations, and escrow updates).
  • Technical information. Our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically process limited technical data needed to operate the service, such as IP addresses and request logs.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: opening and tracking escrows, sharing progress with the people on each transaction, and coordinating work between realtors, associates, and transaction coordinators.
  • To send transactional notifications and emails about escrow activity and invitations.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for advertising, and the app contains no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

How information is shared

  • Within a transaction. Escrow information is visible to the professionals and clients who are parties to that escrow, as described in the app. A realtor’s public profile (name, photo, brokerage, and the other fields they choose to publish) is visible to anyone with their profile link.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers to run the service: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), Resend (email delivery), Expo and Apple (push notification delivery), and GitHub Pages (hosting the web app). These providers process information only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account and transaction information while the account is active, because escrows are business records for the professionals using the service. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing us at the address below. Professionals may also ask us to delete their account and its associated data; we will complete verified deletion requests and confirm by email.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so that each escrow is visible only to its owner, invited team members, and invited clients. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

Children

Clear to Close is a professional tool for real estate transactions. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.
